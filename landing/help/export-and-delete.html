Export and delete your data

Export my data

Open Account in the editor and choose Export my data. The editor builds one zip from the relay, scoped to your account:

  • projects.json: your cloud projects, full documents, in the same shape the editor opens.
  • media-manifest.json: every media asset the relay tracks for you, with the storage path of each part.
  • jobs.json: your job history across server renders, analyses and agent renders.
  • connect-tokens.json: your connect tokens by label and date. Token hashes are never exported, so the file cannot be used as a key.

The manifest lists originals by path. Download any originals you still need from the app while signed in, before you delete the account.

Delete your account

In the same Account panel, the Delete account section asks you to type your email address to confirm. Deletion then runs on the server in a fixed order:

  1. Every file in your relay storage space is deleted.
  2. Your rows are deleted from the project, media, job, command, token and version tables.
  3. Your login is deleted last, so nothing is left pointing at an account that no longer exists.

The panel reports how many files and records were removed.

One consequence to understand before you confirm: CutLab shares its login system with Our Study Home, a study planner run by the same operator. The account you delete here is the same login. Deleting it removes that login for both services, along with the CutLab data listed above.

What deletion does not touch

  • Projects and media cached locally in your browser. Clear the browser's site data if you want those gone too.
  • Files your own Muse downloaded while rendering for you. Those live on that machine, under your control.
  • Copies inside the operator's offline backups. Backups exist so a broken relay can be restored; they are not a user archive and they age out as new backups replace them.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you might come back, export first and simply sign out instead.
