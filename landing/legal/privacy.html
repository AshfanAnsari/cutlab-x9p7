Privacy policy

What the relay holds

  • Account data: your email address and a password hash, held by the shared login system that also serves Our Study Home.
  • Your work: cloud project documents, the media you upload for jobs or cloud projects, and the outputs jobs produce, including finished renders.
  • Job records: what you queued, when, which lane ran it, and the result or error it ended with.
  • Connect tokens: a cryptographic hash, a label and dates. The token itself is never stored.
  • Feedback: reports you send from the editor, tied to your account so the owner can follow up.

The relay is a hosted Supabase project (database plus file storage). Every table and file is scoped to an account, and the scoping is enforced by the database itself.

Where processing happens

  • Server jobs run on the operator's server: media analysis, transcription, separation and renders you send to the server queue.
  • Agent renders run on your own assistant's machine. Your files travel from relay storage to that machine, and the finished render travels back.
  • Prompt edits send your instruction text and the project document to the AI lane in use: your connected assistant, a provider whose key you supplied, or, where offered, the house lane's model gateway. Media files are not sent to the prompt lane.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no sale of personal data, no third-party analytics or trackers on the editor or these pages.
  • No training of models on your projects or media by the operator.
  • No reading of your projects for any purpose beyond running the jobs you queue, keeping the relay safe, and answering feedback you send.

Cookies and local storage

The editor stores your session token, settings and a local media cache in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. That is what keeps you signed in and your work available offline in the tab. Clearing the browser's site data removes all of it. These pages set nothing.

Retention and backups

Relay data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. The operator keeps offline backups of the relay tables so a broken relay can be restored; backups are operational copies, they are not shared, and they age out as newer backups replace them. A deletion therefore disappears from the live relay immediately and from backups as they rotate.

Your rights

Inside the app you can export everything the relay holds for you and delete your account outright, at any time, without asking anyone. These tools are the practical form of the access, correction, portability and erasure rights described by India's DPDP Act and, where it applies, the GDPR. Children

CutLab is a general-audience editing tool and is not directed at children. Accounts are for users old enough to agree to the terms, or supervised by a guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the change will be noted here with a new date before it takes effect.
