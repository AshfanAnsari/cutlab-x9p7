Content policy and takedowns

What may not be made or stored with CutLab

  • Content that is illegal where you are or where the relay operates.
  • Sexual content involving minors, in any form. This carries zero tolerance: the account is removed and the material is reported to the competent authority.
  • Copyrighted footage, music or voices you have no right to use, beyond what fair dealing or a licence actually covers.
  • Impersonation edits meant to deceive: fabricated statements by real people presented as real.
  • Content whose purpose is harassment, threats or incitement against a person or a group.
  • Malware, credential harvesting, or media crafted to attack the relay, the renderer or another user's machine.

Enforcement

The relay is account-scoped and private by design, so enforcement is normally reactive: a credible report, a takedown request, or abuse that endangers the service. Depending on severity the operator may remove specific files, suspend an account, or delete it. Where the law requires it, material is preserved for the authorities instead of deleted.

Takedown requests

If your work appears in CutLab without your permission, write to infographia19@gmail.com with:

  • your name and how you can be reached;
  • what the work is and why it is yours;
  • where it appears: the shared link, project reference or account email involved, as precisely as you can;
  • a good-faith statement that the use is not authorised by you, your agent or the law.

Valid requests are actioned by removing the material from the relay. If you believe a removal was a mistake, reply to the same address with your basis for using the work and the material can be restored while the matter is resolved.

Repeat infringement

An account that repeatedly uploads material it has no right to use loses the account. One warning is the norm for a first, plausibly accidental case; deliberate repetition is not warned twice.
